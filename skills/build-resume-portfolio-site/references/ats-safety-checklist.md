# ATS Safety Checklist

Use this as a final content and structure review, not as a promise of an ATS score.

- Use clear section headings and plain text for role, organization, dates, skills, and contact details.
- Keep abbreviations and their full forms when both are supported and useful, such as `retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)`.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, hidden text, repeated synonyms, and unsupported employer-specific language.
- Confirm that extracted plain text preserves section order and every approved claim.
- Keep project bullets concise enough for a human scan; ATS compatibility does not justify dense paragraphs.
- Treat page count, match percentage, and employer parsing behavior as context-dependent, not universal rules.
- Verify every metric and technology against evidence before final approval.
